MAY WELCOME SCREEN REPLACEMENT
==============================
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Copy app/(auth)/welcome.tsx into may-mobile/app/(auth)/welcome.tsx (replace existing).
3. Copy assets/images/may-welcome.png into may-mobile/assets/images/may-welcome.png.
4. From may-mobile run: npx tsc --noEmit
5. Test: npx expo start --dev-client -c
6. For already installed OTA-enabled APKs with matching runtime:
   eas update --channel preview --environment preview --message "New MAY welcome screen"

This one-screen welcome replaces the prior swiper with the exact approved artwork.
Tap the pictured Next button to open the existing Sign Up screen.
The dots are embedded in the graphic and are decorative only.
No native dependency changes required.
